Manifest Kinetics, LLC

Engineering the Next Generation of Aquatic Performance Hardware

Manifest Kinetics is an early-stage sports technology company based in Davis, California, specializing in data-driven training hardware for competitive swimmers.

Currently in Development

We are currently in the CAD engineering and prototyping phase of our flagship product: a custom robotic sports science device. This system is designed to provide variable kinematic resistance to swimmers while synchronizing 50Hz telemetry data with high-speed camera footage for advanced stroke mechanics analysis.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between high-performance aquatic coaching and precision electromechanical engineering.
